About

Surface Physics Lab

Experimental condensed matter physics at the Department of Physics, IIT Delhi.

Our group, led by Prof. Sujit Manna, is based in the Department of Physics at the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi. We are interested in experimental studies of quantum materials, with a focus on understanding their electronic and magnetic properties.

Through this website, you can learn about our research directions, the people in the group, our publications and academic activities. More detailed descriptions of our current projects are available on the Research page.
